About Me

I am a Hispanic immigrant from Guatemala who came to the United States with a clear goal: to become an engineer and build a meaningful career through hard work and persistence. That journey has shaped how I approach challenges, learning, and growth.

I recently completed my Master’s degree in Mechanical Engineering from Columbia University with a concentration in Robotics and Control, and I hold bachelor’s degrees in Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering from the University of Florida, along with a minor in Mathematics. My academic and project experience has given me a strong foundation in mechanical design, system modeling, robotics, and multidisciplinary engineering work.

I enjoy working on complex problems, turning ideas into real systems, and collaborating with teams to develop practical solutions. My experience includes robotics development, aerospace design, embedded systems, and hands-on fabrication, all of which have strengthened my ability to think critically and approach engineering challenges from multiple perspectives.

I am currently seeking opportunities where I can grow as a mechanical engineer, take on meaningful technical challenges, and contribute to innovative engineering work. I am driven by continuous learning, curiosity, and the desire to make a real impact through engineering.
